Frequently asked questions

What does Intruder Alert do?

It helps protect your phone by attempting to capture a photo when a supported unauthorised interaction is detected and can email that image to your configured address.

Does it work in the background?

The app provides background protection while its protection service is active. Android system restrictions and device settings can affect operation.

Where are alert photos sent?

Captured photos are securely transmitted to the alert server for delivery to the email address you configured.

Does it guarantee my phone cannot be accessed?

No. Intruder Alert is a security assistance tool. Camera availability, network connectivity, Android restrictions and device settings can affect capture and delivery.
